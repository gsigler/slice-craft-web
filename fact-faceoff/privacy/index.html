Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Fact Faceoff is operated by Grady Sigler under SliceCraft. This policy explains the information used by the app and these support pages.

Information the app uses

  • Player identity. The app creates an anonymous account identifier and stores a session on your device. You do not need to provide an email address or password to play.
  • Family and player details. The service stores the family name, join code, player name, and their associated identifiers. You can use a nickname instead of a real name.
  • Rounds and progress. The service stores the operation, game mode, score, mistakes, round duration, practice number when applicable, and time of each saved round. It also maintains streaks, badge progress, and leaderboard records.
  • Optional notifications. If you allow notifications, the service can store a device push token and time zone to deliver reminders and game updates.

How information is used

Information lets the app connect family members, save rounds, display scores, track progress, calculate badges and streaks, and deliver optional notifications. Technical information also helps operate and troubleshoot the service. The app does not include advertising or advertising trackers.

What your family can see

Other members of your family can see your player name and Faceoff scores. Anyone who has your family code can join, so share it only with people you know. Practice results do not appear on the family leaderboard, but the service stores them to track progress and badges.

Service providers

Fact Faceoff uses Supabase for account, database, and server services. It uses Expo to deliver app updates and support push notifications. When enabled, Expo and Apple's push service process device tokens and notification content to deliver alerts.

Network connections disclose technical information, such as an IP address, to the service receiving the request. Service providers may keep operational and security logs. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. These Fact Faceoff web pages do not add analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking scripts.

If you follow an external link from the app, the destination service's privacy policy applies to your visit.

Storage and deletion

Player and game records remain with the service to support your history and family scores until they are deleted. Removing the app or clearing device storage does not automatically remove records from the service and may lose access to your anonymous player identity.

In app versions with account controls, choose Delete my player in About to permanently delete your anonymous account and its player and game records. Other members retain their data. The recorded family creator can choose Delete family on the Family screen to remove all current members' accounts and the family's data. Older families without recorded ownership support individual deletion. If a creator deletes only their own player, the shared family remains for other members and no longer has that creator's deletion permission.

For a question, access request, correction, or help with deletion, email grady@slicecraft.co. We may need information to identify the correct player and verify the request before changing or deleting records. Please do not send a family join code or a child's full name. Service-provider backups or operational logs may retain information after live records are deleted.

Families and children

Fact Faceoff is designed for families to use together. Parents and guardians should help choose player nicknames, manage family codes, and decide whether to enable notifications. If you are concerned about a child's information, contact us using the address below.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use that information to respond to your request. Share only the details needed to explain the issue.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when this policy changes. For support or privacy questions, contact Grady Sigler at grady@slicecraft.co.
