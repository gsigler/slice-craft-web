Everyone has a score to beat.

Choose addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division. Race the clock on facts 1 through 12, or pick a number for focused practice. Faceoff scores count toward your family's weekly standings.

Track your personal bests, build a daily streak, and earn badges as you practice. Create a family and share its word code to play together. No email address or password is needed to play.

Internet access is required for family setup and score syncing. Each device has its own player identity. Anyone with your family code can join, so share it with people you know.
